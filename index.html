A few ground rules

Good work.
Good energy.

New to hackathons? You're in the right place. We care about what you learn and who you build with—not just the final demo.

01

Bring a team—or find one here

Teams can have 1–4 students. Solo builders are welcome, and we'll make space to meet teammates at kickoff.

02

Start fresh at the event

Projects should be created during the 48-hour hackathon. Open-source tools and libraries are absolutely encouraged—just credit them.

03

Make something you can show

Submit a working prototype, a short demo, and a README explaining the problem, your approach, and what's next.

04

Keep it kind and original

Respect the event code of conduct, protect people's privacy, and make sure your team has permission to use any data or assets.

05

Pitch it to the judges

You'll have 3 minutes to demo and 2 minutes for questions. We'll look at creativity, usefulness, execution, and clarity.
